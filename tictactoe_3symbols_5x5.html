🎮 3-Symbol Tic Tac Toe

Welcome to the Challenge!

This is an advanced version of Tic Tac Toe where you'll face a more challenging opponent on an expanded board. The System controls two symbols and will play strategically against you!

📐 Board Size: 5×5 Grid (25 Cells)

✅ Your Advantage: With a 5×5 board and playing first, you have significantly more space to build winning combinations and defend against the System's dual symbols!

How to Play

  • Click on any empty cell to make your move with X
  • The System will automatically alternate between O and Z
  • The System plays strategically to win while defending against you
  • Get three X's in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win!
  • The larger board gives you more opportunities to outmaneuver the System

⭐ Winning Conditions

You Win: Get three X's in a straight line (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) anywhere on the 5×5 board before the System gets three O's or three Z's in a line.
System Wins: Either O or Z gets three in a row at any point in the game.
Draw: The board fills up (25 moves) with no winner.

Strategy Tips

  • 🎯 Control the center: The middle area is strategic for building winning combinations
  • 🛡️ Balance offense and defense: Build your own winning positions while blocking the System
  • 📍 Use the extra space: The 5×5 board gives you room to maneuver that wasn't available in traditional tic-tac-toe
  • 🔄 Think ahead: The System plays both O and Z optimally, so plan multiple moves ahead
  • 💪 You can win: Unlike the 3×3 version, the 5×5 board with optimal play gives the first player (you) a real chance to win!
